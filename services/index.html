Most Common Call

Clogged Drain Repair

So you have a clogged drain and you cannot fix it. Most people try to fix their clogged drain with a plunger or drain cleaner before they call us. Be careful — some drain cleaners can damage your pipes. If you are not able to unclog your drain, give the professional plumbers at Westland Plumbing a call. We have a snaking drain camera that allows us to locate and completely unclog the drain so that it does not re-clog a few days later.

Drain Services

  • Clogged toilet repair
  • Floor drain backup
  • Grease trap repair
  • Basement drain backup

Urgent Repairs

Pipe Leak & Cracked Pipe Repair

Having a pipe leak or cracked pipe in your home or business can be a real problem. When you give your friends a call at Westland Plumbing, we get a plumber out quickly to fix the problem. If you are experiencing a pipe leak, the first thing you will want to do is shut the water flow off to prevent any further damage. Once you have done that, call us and we will dispatch a plumber right away. In winter we handle frozen and burst pipes almost every day.
