What We Do

Plumbing Services in Westland MI

We are your one-stop shop for all plumbing needs — repair and installation for both residential and commercial applications. If you don’t see what you need, just call us.

Drain Cleaning

Clogged drains, sinks, toilets, and floor drains cleared fast. We use a snaking drain camera to locate and completely unclog the drain so it doesn’t re-clog a few days later. No harsh chemicals that can damage your pipes.

Pipe Leak & Crack Repair

A pipe leak can cause serious water damage quickly. Shut off the water first, then call us. We repair and replace all types of pipes — including frozen and burst pipes — and restore your water service fast.

Water Heater Repair & Replacement

From minor repairs to full replacements, we service all makes and models. We work with all major brands including Kohler, Delta, American Standard, and Moen and always provide a written estimate before work begins.

Faucet & Fixture Repair

Leaky faucets, shower and tub faucet repair and replacement, leaking valves, new appliance installation, and more. We work with all major brands and always provide an estimate first.

Garbage Disposal & Installation

Garbage disposal repair and replacement, instant hot installation, and new appliance hookups. We install and service all makes and models with fast, clean work and an upfront estimate before we start.

Emergency Plumbing

Plumbing problems don’t wait for business hours. Westland Plumbing offers true 24-hour emergency service. Call us day or night — we will have a plumber out to your home or business as quickly as possible.
