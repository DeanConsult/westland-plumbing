Our Story

Two Decades of Plumbing Excellence in Westland MI

Westland Plumbing Co is an association of licensed plumbers with over 20 years of experience in the plumbing industry. Our goal is to always provide the highest level of customer service, and our plumbers have been proudly serving Westland for two decades.

All of our plumbers go through rigorous training and re-training on the most up-to-date plumbing advancements, so they are prepared to handle any job when they arrive at your home. Most of our plumbers have 10 to 20+ years of experience in their field.

Our dispatchers are trained to get you help as quickly as possible, and in many cases one of our specialists can give you a general estimate right over the phone. For larger projects we come out, review the work, and provide a written estimate. Our plumbers clean up after every job and leave your home as clean as they found it.

Our Commitment

Honest Plumbers Who Put Customers First

We understand that most plumbing issues are unexpected. We make it our mission to handle every customer’s issue as if it were our own, and we promise to always complete your job as quickly as possible and make it our top priority.

Our estimates are always at fair market value and competitively priced, so our services never break the bank. We offer no-pressure in-home estimates for any size job, and we can often give estimates over the phone for select services. Every plumber we employ will do their best to service your needs at the lowest cost.

In November 2021 we became affiliates with Plumbing News and More, a well respected online plumbing publication. We also offer 24-hour emergency service and evening and weekend appointments for those times when you need a plumber outside normal hours. We want to be your plumber for life.
